VolleyMS is a comprehensive management system for a volleyball clubs. The project is designed to automate the organization's internal processes, including team management, role distribution, task tracking, as well as contract management and monthly financial calculations.

Key features:
Role-Based access - flexible segregation of access rights inside of the club ranging from a regular player to the club owner
Club members - join club requests handling and roster managing processes
Tasks - creating various types of task (daily survey, match, info etc.), assinging them to club members and verifying completion with the ability to apply financial fines/bonueses
Finances - maintaining contract history, creating new agreements and automatically generating monthly payslips (including all the fines and bonuses gathered by the member of a club)
